# Future Roadmap

Potential future capabilities include:

## Workforce Intelligence
- Staffing analytics
- Attendance trends
- Coverage reporting
- Operational dashboards

## AI-Assisted Scheduling
- Availability-aware suggestions
- Conflict detection
- Suggested staff allocation
- Demand forecasting
- Shift coverage recommendations

AI features should assist authorised decision-makers rather than remove required human oversight.

## Integrations
- Payroll
- HR systems
- Accounting
- Workforce management platforms
- Additional communication providers

## Mobile
- Dedicated mobile experience
- Mobile attendance workflows
- Push notifications

## Multi-Site Management
- Multiple care locations
- Site-level administration
- Cross-site workforce visibility

## Compliance
- Expanded audit trails
- Reporting
- Configurable organisational policies
