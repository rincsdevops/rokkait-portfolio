# Business Value

## Before Centralisation

A workforce operation may need to coordinate:

- Staff lists
- Availability
- Leave
- Shift allocation
- Staff communication
- Shift swaps
- Attendance
- Location issues
- Operational records

When these are spread across spreadsheets, messages and manual checks, administrative effort can grow.

## Centralised Workflow

```text
Staff + Availability + Leave
            |
            v
       Rota Planning
            |
            v
      Staff Allocation
            |
            v
       Notifications
            |
            v
         Attendance
            |
            v
      Operational Review
```

## Administrative Time

A simple model for estimating potential benefit is:

**Annual admin hours recovered = weekly hours saved × 52**

### Illustrative Example

If manual rota administration takes 10 hours per week and a digital workflow reduces this by 50%:

- Weekly saving: 5 hours
- Annual saving: 260 hours

This is an illustrative model only. It is not a measured RotaMan performance claim.

## Other Potential Benefits

- Centralised information
- Faster schedule changes
- Reduced spreadsheet dependency
- Consistent approval workflows
- Better role separation
- Location-aware attendance
- Improved operational visibility
- Foundation for future automation
