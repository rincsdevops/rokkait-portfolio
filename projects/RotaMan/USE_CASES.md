# Use Cases

## Care Home

Plan multiple staff shifts, manage leave and availability, allocate workers and record attendance.

## Domiciliary Care

Support workforce scheduling where location-aware attendance and operational visibility are important.

## Nursing-at-Home Services

Coordinate staff schedules and attendance around distributed care delivery.

## Agency Workforce

Combine permanent staff with bank/agency workers and external companies.

## Multi-Shift Organisations

Apply structured scheduling to organisations operating morning, day and night shifts.

## Custom Business Applications

The underlying patterns — RBAC, workflow management, scheduling, notifications and location-aware rules — can be adapted to other workforce-heavy industries.
