# Project Overview

## Project Type
Business-critical workforce management and rota scheduling web application.

## Industry
Healthcare / Social Care / Workforce Management.

## Objective

Centralise rota planning and workforce operations while reducing repetitive administrative activity.

## Business Challenge

Care organisations need to coordinate employees across shifts while managing leave, availability, staff allocation, shift changes, attendance and communication.

The application was designed to bring these activities into a consistent digital workflow.

## Solution Summary

RotaMan provides:

- Workforce management
- Rota planning
- Shift allocation
- Leave management
- Shift swaps
- Attendance
- Location-aware attendance
- Role-based granular access control
- Notifications
- Operational monitoring

## Design Goals

1. Reduce repetitive administration.
2. Improve visibility of staffing information.
3. Provide consistent operational workflows.
4. Apply granular access control.
5. Support location-aware attendance.
6. Integrate external services.
7. Maintain an extensible architecture.
8. Provide a foundation for future automation and analytics.

## Outcome

RotaMan transforms fragmented scheduling and attendance activities into a centralised digital workflow designed for workforce-heavy care operations.
