### RotaMan

**Care Home Workforce & Rota Management Platform**

RotaMan is a bespoke workforce management platform designed for care-home and care-at-home operations.

The platform brings together:

* Rota planning and staff scheduling
* Shift allocation
* Leave management
* Shift swaps
* Attendance management
* Location-aware attendance verification
* Granular role-based access control
* Notifications and operational monitoring

RotaMan demonstrates our ability to transform complex operational requirements into a secure, structured and user-friendly business application.

**Technology:** C#, .NET, ASP.NET Core Web API, Blazor WebAssembly, Dapper, Microsoft SQL Server, JWT/RBAC, Google Maps, Twilio SMS and Linux-based deployment.

3. **Develop** — Build maintainable and secure software or infrastructure solutions.
